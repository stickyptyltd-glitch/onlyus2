Privacy

Last updated 2026-09-05

The short version: this app is built so there's very little to have a policy about. No accounts, no names, no emails, no phone numbers, at any point.

What we don't collect

  • No account, sign-up, name, email address, or phone number — nothing identifies you.
  • No analytics, no ad networks, no tracking scripts, no affiliate links.
  • No cookies used for tracking (any browser storage used is described below, and stays on your device).

What is technically stored, and by whom

Messages are encrypted on your device before they're ever sent. The server (a Cloudflare Worker, storing data in Cloudflare KV) only ever holds encrypted blobs it cannot read — the decryption key never leaves your device. Messages auto-expire from storage over time (matched to your own disappearing-messages setting if you set one, or after 90 days by default otherwise).

What the server operator can technically observe, since a message has to reach the server to be relayed: roughly when a message was sent and its approximate size (content is padded to reduce how precisely size reveals anything). Never what's inside it.

What your hosting provider can see, separate from what we log

This site is served through Cloudflare. Like any website behind any hosting or CDN provider, Cloudflare's own infrastructure sees the IP address of every request as a basic function of how the internet routes traffic — this isn't something this app's own code logs, stores, or has access to, but it's a real, honest limitation worth stating plainly rather than leaving out. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy for how they handle that at the infrastructure level.

What's stored in your browser

Encryption keys and pairing state are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing your browser's site data removes it. Nothing here is sent anywhere in a form we can read.
