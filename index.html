Two ways to pair, for two different risks

Pick whichever fits the two of you — both lead to the same conversation.

In person

Scan each other's code, face to face. The handshake never touches a server, so there's nothing in the middle to trick.

Remotely

Send a link to someone, anywhere. Compare a short safety number afterward to confirm nobody swapped keys along the way.

What using this actually means

01

Every message is sealed with a key that's used once and destroyed — not one shared key covering the whole conversation.

02

No name, email, or phone number, at any point in setting this up or using it.

03

Set a passcode. Get it wrong too many times in a row, and the device wipes itself completely — every pairing, every message, the passcode too.

04

Flag any message to disappear the moment it's read, or set a whole conversation to auto-delete after a set time.

05

Delete a conversation and it's gone for both of you at once — not just hidden on your side.

06

The source is open. What it does is exactly what you can read it doing — nothing is hidden from you either.
